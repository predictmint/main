exit/9to5

A trading journal for short-window Kalshi crypto markets (BTC, ETH, LTC). It prices contracts against a volatility-based fair-value model, only trades when there's a measurable gap between that model and the market's price, and logs every decision — entry price, size, and the exact reasoning — in plain language.

how it works

01

The engine pulls real Kalshi market prices for short-window (15–60m) BTC/ETH/LTC contracts and estimates each contract's "fair value" from the underlying's recent volatility — the same style of math used to price binary/event contracts professionally.

02

When the market's price and the model's fair value disagree by more than a set threshold, it takes a position sized to the size of that gap. When they agree, it does nothing — most of the time, that's the correct move.

03

Every open and close is journaled: the entry price, contracts sized, the model's fair value vs. the market's price, the dollar risk and reward, and the outcome. Nothing is summarized away.

04

Demo mode runs this against real live Kalshi prices with simulated (not real) order fills — so the numbers you see reflect real market conditions, without real money changing hands.
